Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about admissions, learning pathways and student life at Alteora Academy.

What curriculum do you offer?

We provide a broad international curriculum that balances strong academic foundations with practical learning, creativity and personal development.

English is our primary language of instruction, alongside language-learning opportunities that support global awareness.

Ofcourse, we ensure that students who enroll in our school are given the the freedom to ask, learn and receive directions in aspects he/she whats to know

Scholarship and financial-support options may be available for eligible applicants. Please contact our admissions team for the latest information.
